Privacy policy: PC Diagnostics Toolkit

Last updated: November 2, 2026

PC Diagnostics Toolkit is made by NiyiSoft. This policy covers the Windows app, including the Microsoft Store version.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app reads information about your PC (such as processor and memory use, disk space, drive health, temperature, battery and startup programs) only to show you a report on your own screen.

Where your information goes

Nowhere. The app works offline and does not send anything over the internet. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Files the app saves

Reports and plans are saved as files on your PC, in a "reports" folder where you ran the app (or in Documents\PC Diagnostics Toolkit when started from the Start menu). You can delete them at any time. They are only shared if you choose to share them yourself.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at https://github.com/Alameen-26/PC-Diagnostics-Toolkit/issues
